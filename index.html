Mellow Laps Uploader

Mellow Laps Uploader is a private, single-user application used by the owner of the Mellow Laps YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel through the YouTube Data API.

With it, the channel owner can:

  • upload videos to the Mellow Laps channel,
  • list the channel's videos and read their processing status and metadata,
  • update video metadata and visibility (for example, flipping a processed upload from private to public), and
  • delete duplicate uploads.

The application is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only by the channel owner to operate their own channel.
